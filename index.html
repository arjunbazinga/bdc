Before you begin

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. A score here cannot tell you whether you have depression — only a doctor or therapist can do that. If your score is high, or it worries you, please treat that as a reason to talk to someone qualified.

If you are thinking about harming yourself, please reach out now — you can find a helpline in your country.

    What to do with this

    A single score is a snapshot, not a verdict — mood moves around. What is useful is the trend: retake the checklist in a week or two and compare. If your score stays in the moderate range or higher, or it drops sharply, that is worth bringing to a doctor or therapist.
